Clothing wear is a fashion and discipline activity that builds comfort with assigned attire. Whether lingerie, uniforms, or other clothing, wearing what you're told reinforces submission and self-presentation.

### Getting Started

Start with outfits that feel comfortable yet challenging. Even 30 minutes of wearing assigned clothing counts as a valid session.

### Tips for Progress

- Choose outfits that push your comfort zone slightly without causing distress.
- Move around naturally in your clothing — walk, sit, stretch — to get used to the feel.
- Track duration and variety to measure your growing comfort.
