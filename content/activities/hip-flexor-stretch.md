Hip flexor stretching opens the front of the hips, improving flexibility for splits, deep squats, and graceful movement. Tight hip flexors are common — consistent stretching makes a dramatic difference.

### Getting Started

Start with a lunge position, keeping your back straight and leaning gently forward. Hold each side for 30-60 seconds.

### Tips for Progress

- Breathe deeply into the stretch — don't hold your breath.
- Track sets and duration to measure consistency.
- Stretch both sides equally, even if one side is tighter.
