Stripping practice develops the art of seductive undressing — turning removal of clothing into a performance. It combines movement, timing, and confidence into a captivating display.

### Getting Started

Start by practicing with one or two items of clothing. Focus on slow, deliberate movements that build anticipation.

### Tips for Progress

- Practice in front of a mirror to observe your body language and expressions.
- Coordinate movements with music — rhythm enhances the performance quality.
- Develop routines that progress from simple to complex as your confidence grows.
