Weight tracking provides a simple but powerful metric for monitoring your physical progress over time. Consistent tracking reveals trends that daily fluctuations hide.

### Getting Started

Weigh yourself at the same time each day, preferably in the morning before eating. Use the same scale and keep it on a flat, hard surface.

### Tips for Progress

- Don't obsess over daily changes — look at weekly averages instead.
- Combine weight tracking with body measurements for a complete picture.
- Remember that muscle weighs more than fat — the scale doesn't tell the whole story.
