Oral practice builds the fundamental skills for oral service — technique, breathing, and comfort. Regular practice sessions are essential for steady improvement.

### Getting Started

Start with shorter sessions and focus on one technique at a time. Breathe through your nose and keep your jaw relaxed.

### Tips for Progress

- Practice consistently rather than in long, infrequent sessions.
- Track which techniques you work on each session to ensure balanced development.
- Focus on comfort first, then gradually increase duration and complexity.
