Squat holds build leg strength, endurance, and flexibility — essential for both physical fitness and graceful movement. Deep squats engage the entire lower body and core.

### Getting Started

Start with shorter holds — even 30 seconds is a valid beginning. Focus on proper form: knees tracking over toes, back straight, weight in your heels.

### Tips for Progress

- Breathe steadily throughout the hold — don't hold your breath.
- Gradually increase hold time by 15-30 seconds each session.
- Track repetitions to measure your growing endurance.
