Impact reception builds tolerance, endurance, and the ability to stay present through intense sensation. Learning to breathe through impact and find focus is a skill that develops with practice.

### Getting Started

Start with light impact and short sessions. Focus on your breathing — it's the key to managing intensity and staying grounded.

### Tips for Progress

- Communicate clearly about intensity levels before and during sessions.
- Practice breathing techniques: slow, deep breaths help you stay centered.
- Track intensity honestly — building tolerance gradually is safer and more sustainable.

Check out the **[Primal Instincts](/courses/primal-instincts)** course for structured lessons on embracing sensation, or **[Command Compliance](/courses/command-compliance)** for staying still and responsive during impact.
