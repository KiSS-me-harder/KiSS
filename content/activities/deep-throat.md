Deep throat practice builds both physical endurance and mental comfort with deep oral reception. Consistent, gradual practice is the key to progress.

### Getting Started

Start by relaxing your throat and focusing on steady breathing through your nose. Practice with smaller diameters first and gradually work your way up. Never force past pain.

### Tips for Progress

- Breathe through your nose consistently — this helps keep your throat relaxed.
- Start with short sessions and focus on depth gradually, not duration.
- Use lubricant to reduce friction and make practice more comfortable.
