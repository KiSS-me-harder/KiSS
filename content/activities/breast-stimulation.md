Breast stimulation builds sensitivity and awareness, connecting physical sensation with mental submission. Regular practice heightens responsiveness and deepens your connection to your body.

### Getting Started

Start with gentle touch and short sessions. Focus on breathing and allowing yourself to fully experience the sensations without resistance.

### Tips for Progress

- Begin with light pressure and gradually increase intensity as comfort grows.
- Track intensity honestly to gauge your sensitivity development.
- Combine with other sensory activities for compounded effect.
