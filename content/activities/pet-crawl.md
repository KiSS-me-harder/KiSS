Pet crawling is a core pet play activity that builds physical endurance and reinforces the mindset of being a good pet. Crawling on all fours connects body and mind to your pet role.

### Getting Started

Start with short crawling sessions in a safe, comfortable space. Focus on staying on all fours and moving naturally.

### Tips for Progress

- Keep your back flat and move at a steady pace.
- Gradually increase both duration and distance as endurance builds.
- Combine crawling with other pet behaviors like sitting quietly or waiting for commands.
