Self-penetration practice builds comfort, flexibility, and control with anal reception. It's a core activity for progressive anal training.

### Getting Started

Start with smaller sizes and focus on relaxation. Use plenty of lubricant and move at your own pace. The goal is gradual progress, not rushing.

### Tips for Progress

- Relax your muscles completely before inserting — tension is the enemy.
- Gradually increase size as comfort allows, not before.
- Track both duration and size to measure real progress over time.
