Service tasks are practical acts of submission — completing chores, running errands, or attending to others' needs. Each task is an opportunity to demonstrate dedication and usefulness.

### Getting Started

Start with simple tasks — cleaning, organizing, preparing food. Even 15 minutes of focused service counts as a valid session.

### Tips for Progress

- Approach each task with full attention and care — quality matters.
- Track the number of tasks you complete to measure your growing dedication.
- Look for opportunities to serve beyond what's explicitly asked.

### Progressing

Check out the **[Servant Ethics](/courses/servant-ethics)** course for structured lessons on the philosophy and practice of service, or **[Command Compliance](/courses/command-compliance)** for responding to direction.

For a comprehensive path, the **[Obedient](/degrees/obedient)** degree program covers all aspects of service and obedience from beginner to advanced levels.
