Cum drinking is an act of devoted service — consuming from a vessel as a demonstration of submission and willingness. It builds comfort and reinforces the mindset of a willing servant.

### Getting Started

Start with small amounts and focus on the intention behind the act. The mindset of service is what makes this meaningful.

### Tips for Progress

- Approach each session with gratitude and presence.
- Track volume and frequency to measure your growing dedication.
- Combine with other service activities for a complete training experience.

### Progressing

Check out the **[Consumption Technique](/courses/consumption-technique)** course for structured lessons on technique and mindset, or **[Oral Devotion](/courses/oral-devotion)** for broader oral service skills.

For a comprehensive path, the **[Cum Eater](/degrees/cum-eater)** degree program covers all aspects of consumption training from beginner to advanced levels.
