Finger stretching is a foundational anal training exercise that builds comfort, flexibility, and control. Progressive stretching prepares your body for more advanced activities.

### Getting Started

Start with one finger and focus on relaxation. Use plenty of lubricant and move slowly. The goal is comfort, not speed.

### Tips for Progress

- Breathe deeply and relax your muscles before and during stretching.
- Gradually increase from one to two, then three fingers as comfort allows.
- Hold each position for several minutes to allow muscles to adapt.
