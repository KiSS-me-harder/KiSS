Oral endurance training builds the stamina needed for extended oral service sessions. Consistent practice helps you maintain quality over longer periods.

### Getting Started

Start with 10-15 minute sessions and focus on maintaining steady technique. Quality matters more than duration initially.

### Tips for Progress

- Breathe through your nose to conserve energy and keep your jaw relaxed.
- Track your quality rating honestly — it helps identify areas for improvement.
- Gradually increase duration by 5 minutes once you can maintain quality comfortably.
