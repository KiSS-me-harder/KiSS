Chastity lock-up is a core discipline practice that builds mental control and surrender. Tracking your wear time and denials helps you measure progress and build lasting habits.

### Getting Started

Start with shorter sessions — even a few hours is meaningful. Make sure your device fits comfortably and check your body regularly. The key is consistency over intensity.

### Building Discipline

- Set clear goals for each session and log them honestly.
- Track denials separately — each one is a moment of choosing surrender over release.
- Gradually extend wear time as you become more comfortable.
