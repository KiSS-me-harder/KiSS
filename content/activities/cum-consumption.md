Cum consumption is an act of ultimate service and submission — accepting and consuming as a demonstration of devotion. Tracking your sessions helps measure dedication and progress.

### Getting Started

Start with small amounts and focus on the mindset of service. The act is as much mental as physical — embrace it willingly.

### Tips for Progress

- Approach each session with gratitude and willingness.
- Track volume and frequency honestly to measure your growing comfort.
- Combine with oral practice sessions for a complete training experience.

### Progressing

Check out the **[Consumption Technique](/courses/consumption-technique)** course for structured lessons on technique and mindset, or **[Oral Devotion](/courses/oral-devotion)** for broader oral service skills.

For a comprehensive path, the **[Cum Eater](/degrees/cum-eater)** degree program covers all aspects of consumption training from beginner to advanced levels.
