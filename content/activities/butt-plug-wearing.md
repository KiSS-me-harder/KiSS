Butt plug wearing is a foundational anal training activity that builds both physical endurance and mental comfort with anal fullness.

### Getting Started

Choose a plug with a comfortable base and flared stem for safety. Start with smaller sizes and shorter durations — even 10-15 minutes is a valid session. Apply plenty of lubricant and relax your muscles before inserting.

### Tips for Longer Sessions

- Breathe deeply and focus on relaxing your pelvic floor muscles.
- Move around naturally — walking, sitting, light stretching — to get used to the sensation in different positions.
- If you feel discomfort, pause and breathe through it. Pain is a signal to stop, but pressure and fullness are normal.
- Gradually increase duration by 5-10 minutes per session as your body adapts.

### Progressing

As your endurance improves, you can gradually increase both the duration and size of your plugs.
