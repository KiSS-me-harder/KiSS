Face sitting is an advanced service activity that combines submission, endurance, and trust. It requires comfort with restricted breathing and complete surrender of control.

### Getting Started

Only attempt face sitting after building a strong foundation of trust and communication. Start with light pressure and short durations.

### Tips for Progress

- Establish clear signals for communication when your face is covered.
- Focus on breathing through your nose and staying relaxed.
- Gradually increase duration and intensity only as comfort allows.
