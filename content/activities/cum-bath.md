A cum bath is an intense act of submission — receiving and remaining covered as a demonstration of complete surrender. It combines service, endurance, and mental submission into one powerful experience.

### Getting Started

Only attempt cum baths after building a strong foundation of comfort with consumption and service. Start with shorter durations and lower intensity.

### Tips for Progress

- Focus on the mindset of service and gratitude throughout the session.
- Track duration and intensity honestly to measure your growing dedication.
- Practice breathing and staying still — movement can break the experience.
