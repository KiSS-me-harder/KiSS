Body measurements provide objective data on your physical progress — tracking how your physique changes over time through training, diet, and dedication.

### Getting Started

Use a soft measuring tape and measure at consistent points: bust at the fullest part, waist at the narrowest point, hips at the widest part. Measure at the same time of day for accuracy.

### Tips for Progress

- Take measurements weekly rather than daily — changes happen gradually.
- Keep a consistent posture when measuring to ensure accuracy.
- Combine measurements with photos for a complete picture of your progress.
