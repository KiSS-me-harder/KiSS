Sensory deprivation builds comfort with reduced senses, heightening awareness and deepening submission. Removing sensory input forces you to focus inward and surrender control.

### Getting Started

Start with a single sense — a blindfold is the easiest place to begin. Choose a safe, comfortable space and start with short sessions of 10-15 minutes.

### Tips for Progress

- Begin with one sense deprived, then gradually add more as comfort increases.
- Use a safe word or signal if practicing with a partner.
- Track how many senses you deprive to measure your progression.
