Exhibition exposure builds comfort with being seen and vulnerable. Whether through controlled exposure or observing others, this activity deepens your connection to voyeur and exhibition play.

### Getting Started

Start with low-exposure scenarios — wearing revealing clothing at home or observing from a distance. Gradually increase exposure as comfort grows.

### Tips for Progress

- Always prioritize safety and legality — never expose yourself where it's illegal or non-consensual.
- Track your exposure level honestly to gauge your comfort zone expansion.
- Combine exhibition with other activities like clothing wear for compounded effect.
