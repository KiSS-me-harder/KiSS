Split practice builds extreme flexibility in the hips, hamstrings, and groin — essential for graceful movement and advanced poses. Consistent stretching yields steady progress.

### Getting Started

Warm up thoroughly before attempting splits. Start with assisted stretches using blocks or pillows, gradually lowering yourself deeper over time.

### Tips for Progress

- Never bounce or force stretches — hold steadily and breathe into the sensation.
- Track your depth in degrees to measure real progress.
- Practice daily for best results, even if only for 10 minutes.
