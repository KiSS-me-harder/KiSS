# 50 Shades of Grey

## Chapter 1: The Interview

Christian Grey's eyes are the color of melted silver. No, not silver. Steel. Piercing gray steel.

"I'm not a acquiring type, Ms. Steele," he says, his voice low and smooth. "I'm more of a... collector."

My heart races as I sit across from him, trying to maintain my composure during this interview for the Evergreen's yearbook.

## Chapter 2: The Contract

He offers me a contract. Not for employment, but for something else entirely. Something that makes my cheeks burn and my pulse quicken.

"Everything in this contract is optional," he assures me, but his steel-gray eyes suggest otherwise.

## Chapter 3: Exploration

The world he introduces me to is one of pleasure and pain, control and surrender. It challenges everything I thought I knew about desire.

Each session pushes my boundaries further, teaching me that submission can be a form of empowerment.
