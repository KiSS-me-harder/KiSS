---
title: "Collar and Leash"
description: "Being led on a leash while wearing a collar, symbolizing loss of autonomy and total submission."
---

The collar and leash punishment is a powerful visual and psychological discipline. The student is fitted with a collar and attached to a leash, then led by an instructor for a designated period.

While leashed, the student must follow without resistance, maintaining a submissive posture and moving at the pace set by the handler. Speaking is typically prohibited unless given permission. The leash serves as a constant physical reminder of the student's lack of control.

This punishment can be administered privately or in semi-public settings, depending on the severity of the infraction. The visibility of the collar and leash reinforces the social dimension of accountability within the school community.
