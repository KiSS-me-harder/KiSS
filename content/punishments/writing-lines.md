---
title: "Writing Lines"
description: "Repetitive writing of corrective phrases to reinforce lessons through physical and mental discipline."
---

Writing lines is a punishment that combines physical endurance with mental reinforcement. Students must write a designated phrase or apology a specified number of times by hand.

The phrase typically relates to the infraction, such as "I will follow instructions without question" or "I will address my superiors with proper respect." The number of repetitions ranges from 50 for minor offenses to 500 or more for serious ones.

Students must write neatly and legibly; messy handwriting results in starting over. This punishment teaches patience, attention to detail, and ensures the lesson is literally internalized through repetition.
