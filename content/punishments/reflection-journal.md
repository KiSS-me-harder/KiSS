---
title: "Reflection Journal"
description: "Mandatory written self-reflection on misbehavior, its causes, and plans for improvement."
---

The reflection journal requires students to write a detailed account of their misbehavior, analyzing what went wrong and how to prevent it from happening again. This is not a simple apology but a structured self-examination.

Students must address specific prompts: What did I do wrong? Why did I do it? How did my actions affect others? What will I do differently next time? The journal entry must be thoughtful, honest, and at least a specified length.

Instructors review all journal entries and may request revisions if the reflection is deemed insufficient. This punishment targets the root cause of misbehavior by forcing students to confront their actions intellectually, not just physically.
