---
title: "Service Tasks"
description: "Extra duties and chores assigned as punishment, reinforcing that service is both a privilege and an obligation."
---

Service tasks turn punishment into productive labor. Students who misbehave are assigned additional chores beyond their normal duties, such as cleaning classrooms, preparing materials for instructors, or assisting with event setup.

These tasks are deliberately tedious and time-consuming, ensuring the student feels the weight of their misbehavior. However, they also serve a dual purpose: the school benefits from the extra work, and the student learns that their labor is a form of atonement.

Service tasks must be completed to the highest standard. Sloppy work results in redoing the entire assignment, teaching that punishment is not an excuse for lowering one's standards of excellence.
