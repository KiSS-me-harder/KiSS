---
title: "Position Holding"
description: "Maintaining physically demanding positions for extended periods to build endurance and discipline."
---

Position holding requires students to maintain uncomfortable or physically demanding postures for a set duration. Common positions include wall sits, kneeling, standing on one leg, or holding arms extended.

The physical strain of holding a position teaches endurance and mental fortitude. Students must resist the urge to move or break form, even as muscles fatigue and discomfort increases. Speaking or complaining during position holding typically results in the timer being reset.

Duration is proportional to the infraction, ranging from 5 minutes for minor offenses to 30 minutes or more for serious ones. Instructors monitor form closely and will dismiss a student early if proper technique is abandoned, requiring them to start over.
