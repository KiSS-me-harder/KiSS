---
title: "Humiliation Tasks"
description: "Embarrassing assignments designed to break down pride and build humility through controlled humiliation."
---

Humiliation tasks are punishments that use embarrassment as a tool for breaking down ego and building submission. These tasks are carefully calibrated to be uncomfortable without crossing into genuine harm.

Examples include wearing embarrassing outfits during class, reciting apologies aloud in front of peers, or performing menial tasks while maintaining eye contact and a respectful demeanor. The level of public exposure scales with the severity of the original infraction.

The goal is not cruelty but transformation: by learning to accept humiliation gracefully, students shed their pride and become more receptive to guidance. Over time, what once caused shame becomes a source of humility and growth.
