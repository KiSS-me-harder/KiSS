---
title: "Repetition Drills"
description: "Repeating commands, phrases, or exercises until performed flawlessly, reinforcing muscle memory and obedience."
---

Repetition drills punish through relentless practice. The student must repeat a specific action, phrase, or exercise until it is performed to the instructor's satisfaction. This can include drilling proper forms of address, practicing submission postures, or repeating technical exercises.

The key to this punishment is that the student does not know how many repetitions are required. It continues until perfection is achieved, teaching that quality matters more than quantity and that shortcuts are never acceptable.

Repetition drills are particularly effective for correcting behavioral patterns. By repeating the correct behavior hundreds of times, the student begins to internalize it, making proper response automatic rather than forced.
