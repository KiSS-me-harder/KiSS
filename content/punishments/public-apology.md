---
title: "Public Apology"
description: "Making a formal apology in front of peers or instructors to restore trust and accountability."
---

A public apology requires the student to stand before their peers, instructors, or both and formally acknowledge their misbehavior. This punishment emphasizes accountability and the social consequences of one's actions.

The apology must be delivered without excuses, deflections, or minimization. Students must clearly state what they did wrong, who was affected, and how they intend to make amends. Instructors may provide a template or require the student to compose their own.

The public nature of this punishment adds the dimension of social vulnerability, teaching students that their actions affect the entire community, not just themselves. It also allows the community to witness the student's commitment to improvement.
