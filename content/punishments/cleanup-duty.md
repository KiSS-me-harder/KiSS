---
title: "Cleanup Duty"
description: "Assigning thorough cleaning of school facilities as a humble, labor-intensive punishment."
---

Cleanup duty sends a clear message: those who disrupt the school's order will be responsible for restoring it. Students assigned this punishment must clean designated areas to an impeccable standard.

Tasks include scrubbing floors, sanitizing equipment, dusting surfaces, and organizing supplies. The work is thorough and methodical, leaving no corner untouched. Instructors inspect the results and will require re-cleaning if any area falls short of standards.

Beyond the physical labor, cleanup duty teaches humility. Students learn that no task is beneath them and that maintaining the school's environment is a shared responsibility. The punishment transforms misbehavior into tangible contribution.
