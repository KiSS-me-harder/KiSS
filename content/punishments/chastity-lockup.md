---
title: "Chastity Lockup"
description: "Extended chastity device confinement as punishment, denying pleasure and reinforcing submission."
---

Chastity lockup is a punishment where students are placed in a chastity device for an extended period beyond their normal training schedule. The duration is determined by the severity of the infraction.

During punitive lockup, the student has no control over when the key will be returned. This uncertainty amplifies the psychological impact, teaching that privileges are earned through good behavior, not entitlement.

Students must continue their regular duties and classes while locked, demonstrating that punishment does not excuse neglect of responsibilities. The keyholder retains sole authority over release, reinforcing the power dynamic central to the school's philosophy.
