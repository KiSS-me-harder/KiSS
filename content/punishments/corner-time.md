---
title: "Corner Time"
description: "Standing in a designated corner for reflection, promoting stillness and contemplation of misbehavior."
---

Corner time is a simple yet effective punishment that forces students to pause and reflect on their actions. The student must stand facing the corner in a proper posture for a specified duration.

During corner time, no movement is allowed unless instructed. Students are expected to think about what they did wrong and how they will avoid repeating the mistake. Talking is prohibited unless given permission.

Duration scales with the severity of the infraction, ranging from 5 minutes for minor issues to 30 minutes or more for serious violations. The stillness and isolation amplify the mental discomfort, making it a powerful tool for behavioral correction.
