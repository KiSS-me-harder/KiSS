---
title: "Sensory Deprivation"
description: "Restricting senses through blindfolds, earplugs, or restraints to heighten vulnerability and focus."
---

Sensory deprivation removes the student's ability to perceive their environment, creating a state of heightened vulnerability and dependence. This punishment can involve blindfolds, earplugs, hoods, or a combination of sensory restrictions.

Deprived of their usual senses, students become acutely aware of their own thoughts and the consequences of their actions. The isolation forces introspection and makes the student more receptive to instruction when senses are restored.

Sessions range from short periods of single-sense deprivation to extended multi-sense restriction for serious infractions. Students must remain still and quiet throughout, adding a layer of physical discipline to the psychological impact.
