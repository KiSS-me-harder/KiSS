---
title: "Orgasm Denial"
description: "Withholding permission for release after building arousal, teaching control and patience."
---

Orgasm denial is a psychological punishment that targets the most basic desires. The student may be brought to the edge of pleasure through edging or stimulation, only to have release withheld at the last moment.

This punishment can take many forms: complete denial where no release is permitted for a set period, edging denial where the student is repeatedly brought close and then stopped, or conditional denial where release must be earned through exemplary behavior.

The frustration of denied release keeps the student focused on their superiors and the path to redemption. It reinforces that pleasure is a privilege granted by those in authority, not a right to be taken for granted.
