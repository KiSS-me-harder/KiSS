---
title: "Privilege Restriction"
description: "Temporarily removing access to certain classes, clubs, or activities as a consequence of misbehavior."
---

Privilege restriction removes access to activities the student values most. This can include being barred from attending club events, restricted from certain classes, or prohibited from participating in group activities.

The restriction period is determined by the severity of the infraction. During this time, the student must observe from the sidelines, watching others enjoy the privileges they have temporarily forfeited. This amplifies the psychological impact of the punishment.

To have privileges restored, students must demonstrate improved behavior and sometimes complete additional tasks or earn back trust through consistent good conduct. The threat of losing privileges serves as a powerful deterrent against future misbehavior.
