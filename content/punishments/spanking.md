---
title: "Spanking"
description: "A classic disciplinary measure using calibrated impact to correct behavior and reinforce obedience."
---

Spanking is one of the most traditional forms of discipline in the school. It delivers a sharp, memorable sensation that reinforces lessons without causing lasting harm.

Students receive spankings according to a structured scale based on the severity of the infraction. Light infractions result in a warning spanking, while repeated or serious misbehavior escalates to more intense sessions.

Proper form is essential: students must present themselves correctly, maintain their position, and count each stroke aloud unless instructed otherwise. Aftercare includes checking for excessive marks and applying soothing balm when needed.
