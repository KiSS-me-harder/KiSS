---
title: "Cold Shower"
description: "Standing under cold water as a shock to the system, building resilience and mental toughness."
---

The cold shower punishment uses temperature as a tool for discipline. Students must stand under cold running water for a specified duration, enduring the shock and discomfort without complaint.

This punishment serves multiple purposes: it shocks the system back to alertness, builds mental toughness through voluntary discomfort, and provides a physical sensation that is memorable without being harmful. Students must maintain proper posture throughout.

Duration typically ranges from 2 to 10 minutes depending on the infraction. Students are not permitted to turn the water warm or step out early. The cold forces focus and presence, making it impossible to distract oneself from the punishment.
