The Pet Play Club is for those who find joy and release in exploring their animal side. Whether you identify as a puppy, kitty, pony, or another creature, this club provides a space to fully embrace your pet persona.

Sessions include collar and leash training, movement workshops to help you move like your animal, and structured play sessions where you can practice your pet headspace in a supportive environment.

The club organizes pet play parties, outdoor walks in gear, and educational events on topics like pet psychology, gear maintenance, and integrating pet play into everyday life.
