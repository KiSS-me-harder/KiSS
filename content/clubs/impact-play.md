The Impact Play Club teaches the art and science of impact play in a structured, educational environment. Whether you're a recipient looking to explore impact or simply want to understand the practice, this club provides comprehensive training.

Sessions cover the fundamentals of spanking, flogging, and other impact techniques, with emphasis on proper form, safety, and communication. Members learn to read body language, use safewords effectively, and practice proper aftercare.

The club also hosts educational workshops on topics like impact play psychology, marking and its meaning, and how to integrate impact into broader scenes and power dynamics.
