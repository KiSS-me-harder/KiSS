The Service & Obedience Club is for those who find deep satisfaction in serving others and following orders. This club provides structured opportunities to practice submission, develop protocols, and refine your service skills.

Activities include task-based challenges where members complete assignments from their dominants, protocol workshops covering etiquette and manners, and group obedience drills that test your ability to follow instructions under pressure.

The club also focuses on the mindset of service, teaching members how to anticipate needs, find joy in obedience, and maintain a service-oriented attitude even outside of structured scenes.
