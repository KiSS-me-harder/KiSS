The way you speak defines your bimbo persona. This class teaches the speech patterns, catchphrases, and vocal styles associated with bimbo behavior.

Higher pitch, exaggerated enthusiasm, and playful filler words all contribute to the bimbo voice. You will practice until it feels natural.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Vocal pitch and tone adjustment
- Common bimbo catchphrases and expressions
- Practicing natural-sounding bimbo speech
