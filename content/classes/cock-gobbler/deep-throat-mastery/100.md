The gag reflex is your body's natural defense mechanism. This class teaches you to understand it, work with it, and gradually overcome it.

You cannot eliminate the gag reflex entirely, but you can train it to tolerate much more than it naturally would. Understanding the anatomy is the first step.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Anatomy of the gag reflex
- Why the reflex exists and how it triggers
- The difference between gagging and choking
