Removing sight forces you to rely entirely on sensation and trust. This class combines blindfolds with deep throating for an intense experience.

Without visual cues, your other senses heighten. You will learn to find and maintain depth using only touch and your dominant's guidance.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Finding depth without visual reference
- Heightened tactile sensitivity
- Trust and surrender in blind service
