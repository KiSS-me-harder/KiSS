Breathing through your nose while your mouth is fully occupied is the cornerstone of deep throating. This class focuses exclusively on nasal breathing mastery.

You will practice exercises that strengthen nasal breathing under pressure. The ability to breathe calmly while your throat is filled separates amateurs from professionals.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Nasal breathing exercises and drills
- Staying calm when breath is restricted
- Progressive desensitization through breathing
