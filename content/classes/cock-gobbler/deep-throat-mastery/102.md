A tense throat cannot accommodate depth. This class teaches specific relaxation techniques for the throat muscles that allow deeper penetration.

You will learn to consciously relax your pharyngeal muscles on command, creating a smooth passage that welcomes rather than resists.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Throat muscle identification and relaxation
- Progressive throat opening exercises
- Mental techniques for throat relaxation
