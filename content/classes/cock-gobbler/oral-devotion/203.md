Sustaining high-quality oral service for extended periods requires stamina. This class builds the jaw strength and mental focus needed for marathon sessions.

You will practice longer sessions, learning to maintain enthusiasm and technique even as fatigue sets in. Endurance is a trainable skill.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Jaw exercise routines for oral stamina
- Mental focus techniques during long sessions
- Recovery and care for oral muscles
