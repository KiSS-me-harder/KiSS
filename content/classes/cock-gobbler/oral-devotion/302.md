Serving while blindfolded removes your visual cues, forcing you to rely entirely on touch and sound. This advanced class heightens your other senses.

Without sight, your hands and mouth become more sensitive and responsive. You will learn to navigate and adjust using only tactile feedback.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Adapting technique without visual cues
- Enhanced tactile sensitivity training
- Building trust through blind service
