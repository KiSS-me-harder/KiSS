Every master starts with the basics. This class covers the fundamental techniques of oral service that form the foundation of everything that follows.

Proper head movement, lip control, and rhythm are the building blocks. You will practice each element separately before combining them.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Proper head bobbing technique
- Lip sealing and control
- Establishing a consistent rhythm
