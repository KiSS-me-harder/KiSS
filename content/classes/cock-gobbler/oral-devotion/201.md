Maintaining eye contact during oral service creates an intense connection. This class explores the power of visual connection in oral devotion.

Looking up at your dominant while serving shows complete submission and devotion. You will practice maintaining gaze while performing.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Techniques for maintaining eye contact
- The psychological impact of visual connection
- Balancing focus between sight and sensation
