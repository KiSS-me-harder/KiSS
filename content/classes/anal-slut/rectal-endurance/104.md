Theory meets practice in your first extended endurance session. This class puts everything you have learned into action under your dominant's supervision.

You will be filled and expected to maintain composure for a set duration. This is a milestone that proves your commitment and capability.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Guided first extended session experience
- Real-time application of breathing and positioning
- Post-session reflection and improvement planning
