Holding the right position for extended periods is crucial. This class covers the best positions for endurance and how to maintain them without injury.

Different positions put different stresses on your body. You will learn which positions maximize comfort during long sessions and how to shift subtly without losing form.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Endurance-friendly positions and their benefits
- Micro-adjustments to prevent strain
- When and how to request position changes
