Proper lubrication is non-negotiable for safe and enjoyable anal play. This class covers the types of lubricants available and how to use them effectively.

Not all lubricants are created equal. You will learn the differences between water-based, silicone-based, and hybrid options, and when each is most appropriate.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Types of lubricants and their properties
- How to apply lubricant for maximum effectiveness
- Recognizing when you need more lubrication
