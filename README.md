# KiSS

## Planned Features

- Today dashboard, the only screen you need to visit on a daily basis.
- Degrees, where you pick essentially the various aspects of being a sissy you want to go for.
- Courses, the practical blocks that courses are made up of, aimed at helping you achieve your goals.
- Clubs, where you can join and participate in various activities to indulge your sissy experience, focused on having fun.
- Library, where you can access and explore a wide range of resources to support your sissy journey.
- Detention, where you can face consequences for your actions and learn from your mistakes.
- Teachers, where you can interact with and learn from experienced sissy mentors.
- Students, where you can connect with fellow sissies, share experiences, and support each other on your journey.
- Media player, helps you manage and enjoy your sissy media content.
- Flash cards, to help you memorize and reinforce your sissy knowledge.
